How to use
  • Slider — 0% = silent · 100% = original · 200% = boosted
  • 🔇 Mute — Silences a tab without losing your slider position
  • Mute all — Silences every tab instantly (e.g. someone walks in)
  • Master — Scales all tabs proportionally
  • Settings — Saved per website automatically
  • ⚠ DRM — Netflix/Disney+ use copy protection; volume can't be changed
  • ⚡ Boost >100% — May distort audio on some sources
