# Privacy Policy — Fader

**Effective date:** June 2026

## Overview

Fader is a browser extension that controls audio volume on a per-tab basis. Your privacy is straightforward: Fader collects nothing about you.

## Data collected

Fader does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The only information stored is your volume preferences, which are saved locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local` and never leave it.

| Data | Stored | Transmitted |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Volume settings per domain | Locally only | Never |
| Browsing history | No | No |
| Tab content | No | No |
| Personal information | No | No |

## Permissions used

- **`tabs`** — to detect which tabs are playing audio
- **`storage`** — to save your volume settings locally on your device
- **`scripting`** — to inject the audio controller into tabs
- **`activeTab`** — to interact with the current tab
- **`<all_urls>`** — required to inject the audio controller into any tab you choose to control

No data from any of these permissions is transmitted externally.

## Third-party services

Fader does not integrate with any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

The "Buy me a coffee" button links to [buymeacoffee.com/NightIntel](https://buymeacoffee.com/NightIntel), an external site with its own privacy policy. It is only opened if you explicitly click it.
